EMERGENCY ASSISTANCE

Need an ambulance?

If you need emergency transportation, press the Need ambulance button and answer a few questions about your situation.

JeevanSetu will use the information provided to connect you with the designated emergency hotline. The hotline can coordinate with the nearest available hospital and ambulance service to help arrange emergency transportation to your location.

This feature is intended to assist with emergency coordination. Availability and response time may depend on the local ambulance and hospital services.
