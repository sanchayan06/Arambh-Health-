Emergency Dispatch

If you need an ambulance immediately, use the emergency dispatch option below. The system can request your location and share your GPS coordinates with the dispatch system so that the nearest available ambulance can be coordinated.

How Emergency Assistance Works

1

Request Assistance

Tap "Need Ambulance Right Now" to start the emergency request.

2

Location Permission

Your device may ask for permission to access your current location.

3

GPS Location

Your latitude and longitude can be captured and sent to the emergency dispatch system.

4

Hotline Connection

The system connects you with the Medical Officer hotline at 8110.

5

Ambulance Coordination

The hotline operator can use the available location information to coordinate emergency ambulance assistance.

Location Permission

If you deny location access, the emergency call can still be initiated so you can provide your address verbally to the Medical Officer.

Important:

Location access through a web browser requires a secure HTTPS connection and permission from the user. GPS location sharing and automatic dispatch require a connected dispatch backend.
